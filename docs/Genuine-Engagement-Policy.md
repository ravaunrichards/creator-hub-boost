# Genuine Engagement and Participation Rules

## Product promise

Creator Hub Boost is designed to help creators reach real people and receive useful human feedback. It must not sell, guarantee, or manufacture TikTok followers, likes, comments, shares, or views.

## How coin rewards work

- Coins reward eligible work submitted to Creator Hub Boost, such as original content feedback, accessibility feedback, and creative review.
- Coin rewards are paid only after the work is reviewed and approved.
- A participant's reward is never conditioned on following, liking, commenting on, sharing, or repeatedly viewing a TikTok post.
- Participants must be honest about having reviewed the public content and must submit their own relevant feedback.
- Spam, copied feedback, fabricated task completion, multiple accounts, or collusion may be rejected and can lead to account restrictions.
- A creator cannot complete their own campaign.
- Creators can see approved human-feedback responses on their campaign orders.
- This workflow proves that a submitted feedback response exists; it does not prove a particular duration of viewing or guarantee an off-platform metric.

## Why the platform avoids paid follow-for-follow

Real humans can still be incentivized to manipulate a platform's engagement metrics. TikTok's own guidance identifies engagement exchanges and incentives designed to inflate likes, followers, comments, shares, or views as prohibited forms of fake engagement or manipulation. Therefore, Creator Hub Boost rewards the review work within its own platform, not a specified TikTok metric.

## Campaign wording

Use phrases such as “genuine human feedback,” “creator discovery,” and “independent audience research.” Do not describe a feedback package as a guaranteed number of TikTok followers, likes, comments, views, shares, or recommendation placements.

## Launch requirements

Before public launch, add stronger abuse reporting and review tools, account verification, payment webhook reconciliation, privacy and refund policies, retention rules, and integration tests. Never market manual-review queues as automated third-party delivery.
