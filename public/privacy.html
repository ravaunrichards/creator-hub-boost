CREATOR HUB BOOST

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Information we collect

For guest service orders we process the checkout email, public TikTok link, selected service and package, payment references returned by PayPal, order status, and fulfilment notes. For member features we also process account and wallet records needed to operate those features.

2. How information is used

We use order information to create checkout sessions, verify payment, prepare the purchased review, provide private order tracking, prevent duplicate payment credit, and respond to support requests.

3. Payment data

Payments are processed by PayPal. Creator Hub Boost does not request or store your PayPal password or full payment-card credentials. We store payment identifiers and amounts required to reconcile the order.

4. Email and tracking links

When transactional email is configured, we send order confirmation and private tracking links using the email address supplied at checkout. Anyone with a valid private tracking link may view the corresponding order status and published deliverable. Keep the link private.

5. Retention and security

We retain order and payment records for operational, accounting, fraud-prevention, and legal purposes. Private tracking tokens are stored as hashes and expire. We apply access controls and server-side verification, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing

We share the information needed with payment and transactional-email providers to complete the requested service. We do not sell personal information.

7. Choices and contact

You may contact the service operator to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. Some payment and accounting records may need to be retained.
